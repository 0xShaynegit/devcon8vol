Role 05

Supporters and Impact Booths

The booths that help pay for Devcon, and the impact booths that deserve the same visibility. You help them run smoothly.

On this page

  1. The basics
  2. Your team
  3. How you help
  4. Still to confirm

The basics

Supporters are the projects, companies and initiatives that back Devcon. They choose their booths and pay to be at the event, which helps keep Devcon sustainable. In a way, this area is Devcon supporting its supporters.

Impact booths are for very impactful projects and non-profit foundations that probably have no budget but still deserve great visibility. You will find them spread through the venue.

This team works closely with the local production team. Check in at the Volunteer Room with the Coordination Support team.

Your team

Each area has two Volunteer Leads, one for the morning shift and one for the afternoon. Your Volunteer Lead is your first point of contact on shift. Names come from Volunteer Training Session #2 on 16 October 2026 and can change.

How you help

Supporters can need many things, and the Devcon team will add worked examples to the volunteer guide later. Examples given in training:

  • A booth has a TV and needs someone to check it is on, or to find its remote control.
  • A booth just needs support, such as organising the crowd around it.

Anything you cannot fix yourself goes to your Volunteer Lead, who works with the Devcon supporters lead and the production team.

Still to confirm
