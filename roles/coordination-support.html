Coordination Support

The basics

Coordination Support looks after the volunteer team and the community hubs. The Devcon volunteer and community hubs coordinator works on these areas all year round. In Mumbai you check in with this team in the Volunteer Room, and the Volunteer Leads report to it.

Your team

Each area has two Volunteer Leads, one for the morning shift and one for the afternoon. Your Volunteer Lead is your first point of contact on shift. Names come from Volunteer Training Session #2 on 16 October 2026 and can change.

The angel system

The shift tool is the angel system, an open-source coordination tool from the Chaos Communication Congress, where volunteers are called angels. The team has been trying it out since early volunteers met in Mumbai in March. Marco is setting up and adapting it for Devcon, and any volunteer who wants to help improve it is welcome to join.

The community hubs team uses the same tool.

Shifts and swaps

  1. Programming goes liveOnce the programme is published on the Devcon website, the team knows the shifts needed and the room names, and loads them into the angel system.
  2. First scheduleShifts are assigned from the confirmation forms, starting with a basic sheet you can check. The first version uses volunteers with full availability for the week.
  3. Review and swapYou have about a week to confirm your shifts or swap with another volunteer. Swap requests go in the schedule topic of the volunteer Telegram group.
  4. Second callIf spots are still missing, the team calls on people who could only volunteer on certain days.

If hard commitments make a shift impossible, ask for a swap straight away. You can swap a whole week of an area or shift with someone. Roles are assigned, not chosen.

Attendance and standby

  • Attendance is taken for every shift, with arrival and departure times. Until now this was done with sheets and in-person checks, and the team is improving it. Ideas and help are welcome.
  • You may take a second shift if an area needs support. Take a break and lunch, then tell the coordination team you are doing two.
  • A few more applications are being accepted for a standby team at the volunteer headquarters. They plug in wherever someone does not turn up.
